PixelForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

PixelForge is a screen recording and photo/video upscaling app for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

PixelForge itself collects no personal data. We do not create accounts, we do not track you, and we do not send your recordings, photos, or videos anywhere. Screen recordings and upscaled media are created and stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID to show personalized or non-personalized ads. This collection is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings at any time.

Permissions the app uses

  • Screen recording (MediaProjection): used only when you tap record, to capture your screen into a video file on your device.
  • Microphone (optional): used only if you enable microphone audio while recording.
  • Photos and videos access: used only when you choose a photo or video to upscale. Files never leave your device.
  • Notifications: used to show recording and upscaling progress.

Children

PixelForge is intended for users ages 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
